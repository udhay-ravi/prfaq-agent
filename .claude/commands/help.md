  Tips for a Great PRFAQ:
  • Be specific — "users are frustrated" is too vague
  • Include real numbers wherever possible
  • Think from the customer's perspective, not the builder's
  • A good PRFAQ should be understandable by anyone in the company
  • The roadmap should have clear go/no-go criteria between phases
  • Every claim needs supporting evidence
  • Use direct customer quotes (real or representative)

  Typical Workflow:
  1. /start   → Answer questions for all 6 dimensions
  2. /status  → Check your progress anytime
  3. /review  → Get feedback on your draft
  4. /export  → Generate the final document

  Session Files:
  All your work is saved in output/<product-name>/

  • draft.md — Working document (updated incrementally)
  • final-prfaq.md — Exported final document
