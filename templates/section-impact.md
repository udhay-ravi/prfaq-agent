# Section Template: Impact

## Purpose
Quantify the expected outcomes for customers, the business, and the broader strategy. Impact should be measurable and time-bound.

## Target Word Count
300-500 words (plus tables)

## Key Questions to Answer

1. **Business impact:** Revenue, cost savings, market expansion, retention — with numbers.
2. **Customer impact:** Time saved, pain eliminated, new capabilities — with numbers.
3. **Strategic impact:** Competitive positioning, platform value, ecosystem effects.
4. **Success metrics:** 3-5 specific, measurable metrics with targets.
5. **Timeline:** When do we expect to see early signals vs. full impact?

## Format Guidance

- Use tables for customer and business impact (Metric | Current | Target | Timeline)
- Narrative format for strategic impact
- Numbered list for success metrics
- Be specific: "Reduce from X to Y" not "Improve significantly"

## Good vs. Bad Examples

### Bad Example
> "This will significantly improve customer satisfaction and drive revenue growth. We expect strong adoption and positive business outcomes."

**Why it's bad:** No numbers, no timelines, no specific metrics, "significant" and "strong" mean nothing.

### Good Example
> **Customer Impact:**
> | Metric | Current State | Target | Timeline |
> |--------|--------------|--------|----------|
> | Weekly config management time | 12 hrs/week | <1 hr/week | 3 months post-adoption |
> | Drift-related incidents | 2.3/month | <0.5/month | 6 months post-GA |
> | Mean time to detect drift | 4.2 hours | <5 minutes | Immediate |
>
> **Business Impact:**
> | Metric | Current | Target | Timeline |
> |--------|---------|--------|----------|
> | Net new ARR from feature | $0 | $2.4M | 12 months post-GA |
> | Enterprise tier retention | 87% | 93% | 12 months post-GA |
> | Expansion revenue (multi-cloud upsell) | $800K/yr | $2.1M/yr | 18 months |
>
> **Success Metrics:**
> 1. 80% of Private Preview customers actively using daily within 30 days
> 2. NPS score of 50+ from Public Preview participants
> 3. Reduce drift-related support tickets by 60% within 6 months of GA
> 4. 25% of Enterprise customers adopt within first year
> 5. Zero drift-related P1 incidents for CloudSync users

**Why it's good:** Every metric has a current state, target, and timeline. Business impact ties to revenue. Success metrics are specific and measurable.

## Checklist

- [ ] Customer impact has at least 3 metrics with current/target/timeline
- [ ] Business impact has revenue or cost numbers
- [ ] Strategic impact explains competitive positioning
- [ ] 3-5 success metrics with specific, measurable targets
- [ ] Timeline for when impact is expected (early signals + full impact)
- [ ] Metrics connect logically to the problem and solution
