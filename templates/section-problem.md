# Section Template: Problem Statement

## Purpose
Define the customer problem clearly and specifically. This is the foundation of the entire PRFAQ — if the problem isn't compelling, nothing else matters.

## Target Word Count
300-500 words

## Key Questions to Answer

1. **What is the specific customer problem?** Describe it from their perspective — what are they trying to accomplish, and where does it fail?
2. **Who is the target customer?** Define a specific persona with role, industry, company size, and technical level.
3. **How do customers solve this today?** What workarounds, tools, or manual processes do they use?
4. **How often and how severe?** Frequency (daily/weekly/quarterly) and impact (annoyance vs. blocker vs. revenue loss).
5. **What's the single-sentence problem statement?** The "elevator pitch" version.

## Format Guidance

- Lead with the customer persona, then the problem
- Use concrete examples, not abstract descriptions
- Quantify wherever possible (time wasted, money lost, error rates)
- Include a "day in the life" scenario if helpful

## Good vs. Bad Examples

### Bad Example
> "Users have difficulty managing their cloud infrastructure. It's complex and time-consuming. Many companies struggle with this."

**Why it's bad:** Vague persona ("users"), unspecific problem ("difficulty"), no quantification, no workarounds mentioned.

### Good Example
> "Platform engineering teams at mid-size SaaS companies (50-500 engineers) spend an average of 12 hours per week manually synchronizing infrastructure configurations across AWS, GCP, and Azure. When configurations drift, it leads to production incidents — our data shows these teams experience 2.3 drift-related outages per month, each costing an average of $15,000 in engineering time and customer impact. Today, teams rely on a patchwork of custom scripts, spreadsheets, and tribal knowledge to track configurations, but these break down as the team scales past 10 services."

**Why it's good:** Specific persona, quantified time/cost, frequency data, clear workaround description, scale trigger identified.

## Checklist

- [ ] Persona is specific enough to identify a real person
- [ ] Problem is described from the customer's perspective
- [ ] Current workarounds are documented
- [ ] Frequency and severity are quantified
- [ ] One-sentence version is compelling and clear
- [ ] No solution is mentioned in this section
