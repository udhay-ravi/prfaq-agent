# Section Template: Evidence

## Purpose
Validate that the problem is real, significant, and worth solving. Evidence separates conviction from wishful thinking.

## Target Word Count
300-500 words (plus tables and quotes)

## Key Questions to Answer

1. **Quantitative data:** What metrics, surveys, or usage data prove the problem exists?
2. **Qualitative data:** What have customers actually said? Direct quotes from interviews, tickets, reviews.
3. **Market research:** What does the competitive landscape show? Market size?
4. **Internal signals:** Feature requests, escalations, lost deals, NPS data?
5. **Disconfirming evidence:** What data, if found, would make you reconsider?

## Format Guidance

- Use tables for quantitative data (Metric | Value | Source | Date)
- Use blockquotes for customer quotes with attribution
- Separate quantitative from qualitative evidence
- Cite sources for all data points
- Include date/recency of data

## Good vs. Bad Examples

### Bad Example
> "We've heard from many customers that this is a problem. Our surveys show significant demand. The market is growing."

**Why it's bad:** No specific numbers, no direct quotes, no sources, "many" and "significant" are meaningless.

### Good Example
> **Quantitative:**
> | Metric | Value | Source |
> |--------|-------|--------|
> | Support tickets mentioning config drift | 847/month (up 34% YoY) | Zendesk, Jan 2025 |
> | Customers citing multi-cloud as #1 challenge | 67% of Enterprise tier | Annual survey, Q4 2024 (n=312) |
> | Average incident resolution time for drift | 4.2 hours | PagerDuty data, last 6 months |
>
> **Customer Voice:**
> > "I have three engineers who spend half their time just making sure our AWS and GCP configs match. It's the most expensive copy-paste job in the company." — Sarah Chen, VP Engineering at DataFlow (Series C, 200 engineers)

**Why it's good:** Specific numbers with sources and dates, trend data, direct customer quote with attribution and context.

## Checklist

- [ ] At least 3 quantitative data points with sources
- [ ] At least 2 direct customer quotes with attribution
- [ ] Competitive landscape analysis included
- [ ] Internal signal data referenced
- [ ] All data points have sources and dates
- [ ] Disconfirming evidence or counter-arguments addressed
