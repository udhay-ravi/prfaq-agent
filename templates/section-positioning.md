# Section Template: Product Positioning

## Purpose
Define how the product is positioned in the market — who it's for, what category it competes in, how it's differentiated, and what the core messaging should be. This section is AI-generated based on the Problem, Solution, and Evidence dimensions.

## Target Word Count
400-600 words

## Key Elements

1. **Positioning Statement** — Follow the standard framework:
   "For [target customer] who [statement of need/problem], [product name] is a [product category] that [statement of key benefit]. Unlike [primary competitive alternative], our product [statement of primary differentiation]."

2. **Category Definition** — What market category does this product compete in or create?

3. **Value Proposition** — The core promise to the customer in 1-2 sentences.

4. **Key Messages (3-5)** — Messaging pillars for marketing and sales. Each has a headline and 1-2 supporting sentences.

5. **Target Segments** — Primary, secondary, and tertiary customer segments with prioritization rationale.

6. **Competitive Moat** — What makes this product defensible over time? (network effects, data advantages, switching costs, brand, technology, etc.)

7. **Brand Voice & Tone** — How should this product communicate with its audience?

## Format Guidance

- Positioning statement should use the standard "For...who...is a...that...Unlike..." framework
- Key messages as a numbered list with bold headlines
- Target segments as a table: Segment | Description | Urgency | Rationale
- Competitive moat as a narrative paragraph with specific mechanisms

## Good vs. Bad Examples

### Bad Example
> "Our product is for everyone who needs better productivity. We're different because we're better and faster. Our value prop is that we help people do more."

**Why it's bad:** Generic target ("everyone"), no specific category, no framework, "better and faster" is not differentiation, no competitive moat identified.

### Good Example
> **Positioning Statement:** For platform engineering leads at mid-size SaaS companies who lose 12+ hours per week to manual multi-cloud configuration management, CloudSync is an automated infrastructure synchronization platform that eliminates configuration drift in real time. Unlike Terraform and cloud-native tools that only manage provisioned resources, CloudSync continuously monitors and auto-remediates drift from any source across all cloud providers.
>
> **Category:** Automated Multi-Cloud Infrastructure Synchronization
>
> **Value Proposition:** Stop babysitting infrastructure configs. CloudSync keeps your multi-cloud estate in sync automatically so your platform team can build instead of firefight.
>
> **Key Messages:**
> 1. **Zero-effort synchronization** — Connect your clouds in 5 minutes and never manually compare configs again
> 2. **Real-time drift detection** — Catch configuration changes within 60 seconds, before they cause incidents
> 3. **Policy-driven auto-remediation** — Define your rules once; CloudSync enforces them automatically
>
> **Competitive Moat:** Cross-cloud configuration graph that improves with usage — the more services connected, the more intelligent the drift detection becomes. Switching costs increase as teams build policy libraries and workflows around CloudSync.

**Why it's good:** Specific target, clear category, concrete value prop, messages tied to specific capabilities, moat explains defensibility mechanisms.
